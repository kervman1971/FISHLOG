# 🎣 C'mon Bell!

A shared fishing journal for our group. Log catches, save spots, plan trips around the bite — everyone sees the same data, in real time.

---

## Adding it to your phone

Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and add it to your home screen so it feels like a real app.

**iPhone:**
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"

**Android:**
1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap the **⋮** menu
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"

---

## What it does

- **Log a catch** — species, weight, length, lure, photo, notes, and your GPS spot. Weather and solunar conditions are filled in automatically.
- **Edit anytime** — tap any catch to fix a detail or update it later.
- **Photos** — attach a photo per catch, and pinch or double-tap to zoom in on any saved photo.
- **Spots** — save your fishing holes with coordinates, water type, and access notes. View them all together on a map.
- **Trips** — every outing is automatically grouped by day across the whole group, with total fish, species mix, and duration.
- **Forecast** — a 7-day solunar outlook (moon phase, major/minor bite windows, sunrise/sunset) so you can plan ahead, plus a live "good time to fish right now" indicator.
- **Stats** — leaderboard, top species, best lures, and hot spots across everyone's catches.
- **Tactics** — simple tips pulled from your own group's catch history.

---

## How the data works

- Everything you log is **shared live** with the group — when someone logs a catch, it shows up for everyone within a few seconds.
- Your **name** is asked once and saved only on your own phone, so your catches are attributed correctly without asking again.
- Photos are stored securely and shown to everyone in the group.
- An internet connection is needed to sync — if you're out of signal when you log a catch, save it again once you're back in range.
