# 🎣 Fishing Log

A simple fishing journal for our group. Log catches, save spots, and see who's winning.

---

## How to use it

Open the link in your phone browser and bookmark it to your home screen so it feels like an app.

**To add it to your iPhone home screen:**
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"

---

## What it does

- **Log catches** — species, weight, length, lure, weather, photo and notes
- **Save spots** — name, GPS coordinates, water type and access
- **Dashboard** — quick stats and recent patterns at a glance
- **Stats** — leaderboard, top species, best lures and hot spots
- **Tactics** — simple tips pulled from your own catch history

---

## A few things to know

- Your data is saved on **your own phone** — it doesn't sync between people
- If you clear your browser data your catches will be lost, so use the Export button to back up now and then
- The app works best in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android
- It's a private link — just don't share it publicly and it stays between us
